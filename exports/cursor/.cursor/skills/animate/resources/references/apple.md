# Optional Apple interaction and material profile

Adapted from apple-design. Activate for Apple-platform conventions or a requested fluid/physical Apple-like interaction, not as the universal appearance of every website.

Give immediate input feedback; keep direct manipulation attached to the finger/pointer and its grab offset. Preserve presentation state and velocity when interrupted, project momentum to choose a useful snap target, and use progressive resistance rather than abrupt boundaries. Springs support continuous/interruptible behavior and expressive character. Choose damping, bounce, and overshoot for the desired feel, product identity, and gesture; critically damped and playful spring treatments are both available.

Preserve spatial continuity: related entry/exit paths and trigger-aware origins for anchored surfaces. Do not lock out input while a transition runs. Decompose independent axes if required by the gesture/runtime. Typography uses appropriate size, weight, tracking, leading, optical sizing, user text scaling, and readable grouping; a system font is a useful default rather than a requirement.

Use materials for hierarchy, atmosphere, depth, and brand expression while preserving legibility. Provide solid/stronger backgrounds for reduced transparency or increased contrast where supported, and gentler alternatives for reduced motion. Animate materials when they serve the direction; profile complex filters and choose efficient layering or crossfades when they retain the intended effect. A blur radius from a sample is not a measured performance guarantee.

Sound/haptics, if the platform and task support them, should match a meaningful causal event and visual feedback. Never rely on them alone. Keep wayfinding, specific labels, undo/recovery, and familiar interaction patterns aligned with the product. No material effect or Apple aesthetic supersedes the shared policy or the project's established identity.
